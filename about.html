About StudentHub

StudentHub is a student management portal that brings important academic information together in one place.

Students can access their timetable, attendance, assignments, notices, examination results, university events, profile information and other useful resources through a single platform.

The main goal of StudentHub is to provide a clean, simple and user-friendly experience for students.

What StudentHub Provides

Everything students need for managing their academic activities in one place.

Timetable

View your weekly class timetable and easily keep track of lectures and laboratory sessions.

Attendance

Check your subject-wise attendance and monitor your overall attendance percentage.

Assignments

View assignments, check deadlines and submit your assignments through the portal.

Notices

Stay updated with important university and academic announcements.

Results

View semester-wise academic results and download your result as a PDF.

University Events

Discover upcoming university events, activities, workshops and competitions.

Student Project

StudentHub is developed as an academic project with the goal of creating a practical student portal that solves common academic management problems.

The project can be further improved by adding authentication, database integration, notifications, online assignment submission, result management and other useful features.

Made for students, by students.
